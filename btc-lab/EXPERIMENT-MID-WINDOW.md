# mid-window-v1 — frozen forward PAPER specification, 2026-09-24

This is a transparent new hypothesis, not a reproduction of Mitch's undisclosed algorithm or a calibrated probability model. No backtest profitability is claimed. It adds an independent $500 virtual account, not live capital. Existing early/late/value accounts, thresholds and losses remain unchanged.

User-specified constraints: first look at elapsed 180 seconds, last buy at elapsed 420 seconds inclusive; execution prices 50–80 cents inclusive. Arrival after 420 seconds cannot buy. At 13:00 market start this means 13:03:00–13:07:00. No sales at or after elapsed 600 seconds; no new buys after 420. Official settlement/redemption is still processed after the cutoff.

Additional preregistered research hypotheses (not user claims): follow the leading Chainlink TWAP60 side only when its direction persisted over at least 30 seconds (>=20 observations, no gap >5s), its net movement over that interval agrees, absolute existing volatility-normalized distance z >=1, and executable spread <=3 cents. Both books must be fresh. z uses the existing historical feature formula and is NOT interpreted as a probability. Do not transfer the late-horizon logistic model into this interval.

Full-notional $5 FOK-style buys use new arrival depth after 250ms plus collection latency, 50% displayed-depth haircut, min size/tick checks and a fixed limit <=80c. Every fill price must be >=50c. One position per market and one open/pending position per account. Existing 1.1% all-in entry cap, 3% rolling-day gross loss budget, 6% rolling-week gross loss budget and 8% realized drawdown budget apply including prospective full loss. The experiment may correctly stop trading. Never reset its losses to restart it.

Exits: evaluate executable full-sale bid proceeds after sale fees against entry cost plus entry fees. Trigger at net +10% of entry basis, net -20%, or elapsed >=590s. These thresholds are arbitrary precommitted research values, not optimized or guaranteed. Request new books after 250ms. Sale floor is the lowest decision-fill bid; no chasing lower bids. Require the full remaining position at permitted depth/minimum/tick. A rejected FOK sells nothing; inventory remains exposed. Retry on later valid cycles before 600s. A trigger at 599.9s cannot execute at 600.2s. If no full exit succeeds, retain the entire risk and settle only on the official winner. A -20% trigger is NOT a maximum loss. Partial-fill venue behavior and order queue are not modeled; this is an explicit FOK approximation.

Ledger: CLOSED identifies an early simulated sale. Gross sale proceeds are payout, exit_fee is separately recorded, PnL=payout-cost-entry_fee-exit_fee; cash receives gross proceeds minus exit_fee. Both fees are reported. An atomic sale event is idempotent and cannot receive official redemption again. Existing positions migrate with exit_fee=0. No historical trades are fabricated.

Comparison: compare all strategies only over the common forward period after this experiment's first recorded decision, including no-trade windows, outages and risk pauses. Lifetime totals started on different dates are not fair competitors. Preserve the original baselines. A difference versus early-v1 confounds time, price, signal and exit rules; it does NOT prove 3–7 is optimal. Isolating timing requires matched-input replay or separate controlled variants later. Daily files contain first_recorded_decision_at and period trades for that purpose.

Acceptance: no live promotion. Freeze v1 while collecting new independent windows; examine daily-block uncertainty, net PnL, realized and open risk, coverage and execution stress. Require positive untouched net evidence robust to worse fills and not dominated by a few wins; if uncertainty includes no edge, retain INCONCLUSIVE. Do not optimize many alternatives on this forward set. A follow-up strategy receives a new version and new evaluation period. 100 USDC live feasibility is separate: $5+fees is >5% of that capital.

Validation: controlled Python tests cover migration, entry boundaries/prices, noise/gaps, delayed cutoff crossing, both sale fees, fixed-price/depth rejection, atomic failure, restart and no duplicate settlement. These are correctness tests, not a forward-performance record. Mac deployment requires the installer and subsequent v0.3.0 export confirmation.
